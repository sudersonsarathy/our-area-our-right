No. 26 · 2 Oct 2026

Unauthorised Excavation of a Public Road and Private Inlet into the New Storm Water Drain, Judges Colony, Noombal

Received
Portal: CM Helpline · Local body: Thiruverkadu Municipality · Type: Roads / Repairs – MAWS · Attachments: 2 PDFs (photograph; letter to the association with certified chat extract)
Grievance ID #19243699
Update (2 Oct): On or about 5 Sep a public road opposite Justice Square Apartment was cut and a pipe laid as an inlet into the newly built storm water drain. The petition states fairly that this is not a house connection — the resident says it drains standing water from the road and that he paid for it himself — but the road vests in the Municipality under s.61 of the TN District Municipalities Act 1920 and only the Municipality can permit the cut or the inlet. In the residents’ group that resident said the work “has been already informed to association” and told the petitioner to “complain to govt not in this group”; no municipal permission was ever claimed, and the petitioner was removed from the group 45 minutes later. A letter to the association sent 8 Sep (courier AWB 26017100093937) was refused and returned; re-sent 10 Sep to the registered address, no reply. Asks for the permission or action, a Municipal Engineer inspection of the inlet while still exposed, an inspection of the rest of the drain for other private connections, and a written notice to residents that only the Municipality can authorise such work.
